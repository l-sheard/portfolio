## The idea

Popular TV competitions like *The Traitors* are built around social deception — some players know things others don't, and the game rewards convincing lies. I wanted to know whether LLM agents, dropped into the same kind of setup, would develop similar deceptive strategies.

## What I built

I put multiple LLM agents against one another in a simulation of the show, giving some agents hidden "traitor" roles and observing how they reasoned about trust, suspicion, and deception across rounds of play.

## Outcome

The investigation into the agents' social reasoning and deceptive abilities placed 2nd in the MSc category at the National Lovelace Colloquium 2026 — the UK's colloquium for women and non-binary computing students to present their research.
