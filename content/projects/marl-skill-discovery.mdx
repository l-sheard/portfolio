## Overview

My dissertation for the BSc part of my integrated master's, awarded 1st Class Honours (75%). The project investigates skill discovery in multi-agent reinforcement learning — how a team of cooperative agents can automatically discover reusable behavioural "skills" rather than relying purely on flat, single-objective policies.

## What I built

I developed a novel graph-based skill discovery algorithm for cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning, designed to let agents identify and reuse structured sub-behaviours across tasks.

## Evaluation

I implemented and tested the algorithm against two other baseline algorithms, across two different test environments, to evaluate how well it generalised compared to existing approaches.
